## ⚖️ Legal & Risk Mitigation

### Entity Structure

**Recommended**: Delaware C-Corp (for future fundraising, acquisition)

**Alternative**: LLC (simpler, pass-through taxation)

**Considerations**:
- Liability protection (code bugs, security issues)
- IP ownership (who owns the agent's output?)
- Tax optimization (R&D credits, depreciation)

### Contracts & Agreements

**Required**:
1. **Terms of Service** (customer agreement)
2. **Privacy Policy** (GDPR, CCPA compliance)
3. **Service Level Agreement** (SLA for uptime, turnaround)
4. **Data Processing Agreement** (DPA for EU customers)
5. **IP Assignment** (customer owns refactored code)

**Optional**:
- Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA for enterprise)
- Master Services Agreement (MSA for white-label)
- Revenue Share Agreement (for trading strategies)

### Liability & Insurance

**Risks**:
- Code bugs introduced by refactoring
- Security vulnerabilities missed in audit
- Downtime, data loss, service interruption
- IP infringement (if agent copies code)

**Mitigation**:
1. **Errors & Omissions Insurance** ($1M-$5M coverage)
2. **Cyber Liability Insurance** ($1M-$5M coverage)
3. **Limitation of Liability** (in ToS, cap at 12 months fees)
4. **Indemnification** (customer indemnifies for their code)
5. **Warranty Disclaimer** ("as-is" service, no guarantees)

**Cost**: $5K-$20K/year for insurance

### Compliance

**Data Privacy**:
- ✅ GDPR (EU): Data minimization, right to deletion
- ✅ CCPA (California): Opt-out of data sale
- ✅ SOC 2 Type II (enterprise requirement)

**Security**:
- ✅ Encryption at rest (AES-256)
- ✅ Encryption in transit (TLS 1.3)
- ✅ Access controls (RBAC, MFA)
- ✅ Audit logging (all actions tracked)

**Export Control**:
- ⚠️ AI/ML models may be export-controlled (ITAR, EAR)
- ⚠️ Consult trade lawyer if selling to non-US customers
